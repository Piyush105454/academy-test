Two things I have not settled. Row 1 (प्रभाती दंग से vs प्रभावी ढंग से) sits at 0.83 similarity, just under the 0.85 threshold, so it counts as wrong. Two real letter errors, so “wrong” is probably right — but a verdict that turns on 0.02 means that threshold is a guess, not a measurement. It needs a teacher marking ~50 real pages beside it.

And abstaining is only fair if the abstain rate is not systematically higher for some students. A child with messier handwriting getting fewer scored rows every week is being failed differently, not fairly. fairness_report() reports this per group — nobody has looked at it on real data yet.
